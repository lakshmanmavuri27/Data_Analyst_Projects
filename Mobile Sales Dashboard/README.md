# 📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze mobile phone sales data and understand sales performance across different brands, mobile models, cities, months, payment methods, and customer ratings.

The dashboard provides an overview of key sales metrics and uses interactive visualizations to explore sales trends, product performance, customer preferences, and transaction patterns.

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- **Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated from mobile sales.
- **Total Quantity:** Total quantity of mobile units sold.
- **Total Transactions:** Total number of sales transactions.
- **Average:** Average sales value.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

1. **Sales by City:** Geographical representation of mobile sales across different cities.
2. **Total Quantity by Month:** Monthly analysis of mobile units sold to identify sales trends.
3. **Top 3 Sales by Mobile Model:** Comparison of the three highest-performing mobile models based on sales.
4. **Transaction by Payment Method:** Distribution of transactions across UPI, Debit Card, Cash, and Credit Card.
5. **Customer Rating:** Analysis of customer ratings from 1 to 5.
6. **Total Sales by Day Name:** Comparison of sales performance across different days of the week.
7. **Brand-wise Sales Analysis:** Comparison of total sales and total quantity across mobile brands.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)

The dashboard includes interactive filters to explore sales data based on:

- Mobile Model
- Payment Method
- Brand
- Day Name
- Month

These filters allow users to analyze specific segments of the sales data.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.
- **DAX:** Creating measures and calculating key performance indicators.
- **Data Modeling:** Organizing data fields for analysis.

## 💡 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall mobile sales performance.
- Compare sales across different mobile brands and models.
- Identify monthly sales and quantity trends.
- Analyze city-wise sales distribution.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Evaluate customer ratings.
- Identify sales patterns across different days of the week.

## 📈 Project Outcome

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that brings together mobile sales KPIs and multiple visualizations in a single report. It enables users to explore sales performance, compare brands and models, and analyze customer purchasing patterns using interactive slicers.
