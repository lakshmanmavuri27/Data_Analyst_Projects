# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance across products, categories, regions, customers, and sales channels.

The project involved data validation, analysis, database integration, and dashboard development to identify revenue trends, profitability, and business performance.

## Tools & Technologies Used

- **Microsoft Excel:** Initial data analysis and KPI calculations.
- **Python (Pandas):** Data inspection and validation, including missing value and duplicate checks.
- **MySQL:** Sales data storage, querying, and integration with Power BI.
- **Power BI Desktop:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures for revenue, profit, quantity, orders, and profit margin.

## Dashboard KPIs

 Total Revenue 
 Total Orders 
 Total Quantity 
 Total Profit 
 Profit Margin 

## Dashboard Features

- **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Analyzed monthly sales performance using a line chart.
- **Profit by Region:** Compared total profit across different regions.
- **Profit Margin by Category:** Visualized profitability across product categories.
- **Revenue by Sales Channel:** Analyzed revenue distribution across Online, Distributor, and Retail channels.
- **Top 5 Products by Revenue:** Used a treemap to identify the highest revenue-generating products.
- **Top 5 Customers by Revenue:** Created a ranked customer table with conditional data bars.

### Interactive Filters

Implemented slicers for:
- Month
- Region
- Category
- Sales Channel
- Order Date Range

## Key Insights

- **Laptop** generated the highest product revenue at approximately $14.9M.
- **North Region** recorded the highest total profit at approximately $1.8M.
- **Online Sales** contributed approximately $12.64M, representing 41.6% of total revenue.
- **September** recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $5.2M.
- **Customer CUST0140** generated the highest revenue among the displayed top five customers, approximately $1.05M.
- The overall profit margin was **26.8%**.
